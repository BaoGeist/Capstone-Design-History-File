# Superseded Approaches Log

## Purpose

This document records approaches, designs, or solutions that were considered but ultimately not selected. Preserving these records is important for:
- Understanding the design evolution
- Preventing re-evaluation of rejected approaches
- Showing evidence of iteration

## Record Format

Each superseded approach should be documented with:
1. What was proposed
2. Why it was considered
3. Why it was rejected
4. What evidence led to the decision
5. When it was superseded
